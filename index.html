About Home & Nanny Care by Nexim Healthcare

Established as a specialized division of Nexim Healthcare Consultants Inc., Home & Nanny Care was founded on a simple yet profound mission: to bring hospital-grade reliability, rigorous vetting, and compassionate management directly into family homes across Ontario and Alberta.

Whether you need an experienced infant nanny or professional home care support for an aging loved one, our agency handles the entire lifecycle of care. From thorough police background checks and clinical interviews to seamless scheduling and transparent billing, we ensure families enjoy absolute peace of mind.

Our 3-Step Managed Support Model

1. Initial Consultation

We meet with your family to map out your home care, nanny, or household support schedules.

2. Hand-Picked Matching

Our management team selects the ideal vetted home care professional or nanny from our roster to match your needs.

3. Simple Managed Billing

Pay through convenient, all-inclusive invoices via Interac e-Transfer, Cheque, or Direct Payment while we handle compliance and oversight.
